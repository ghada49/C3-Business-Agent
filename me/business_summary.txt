CedarBridge Learning is a fictional Lebanon-based online tutoring studio founded in 2024. Its mission is to make STEM learning approachable through bilingual, individually tailored instruction for Grades 7–12. Its unique approach combines a free 20-minute introductory consultation, customized tutor matching, and a progress note after every four completed paid sessions. This is a teaching assignment simulation, not an actual business.

CedarBridge offers live online Mathematics, Physics, and beginner Python programming tutoring for Grades 7–12, plus Mathematics and Physics exam preparation for Lebanese-school programs. Lessons are on Zoom in English or Arabic. The fictional team is Lina Daher (co-founder and Academic Director, focuses on learning plans) and Karim Mansour (co-founder and Operations Lead, coordinates tutoring inquiries). CedarBridge does not offer Chemistry, university courses, a physical tutoring location, or guaranteed exam grades.

A 60-minute individual lesson costs USD 25. A 60-minute small-group lesson costs USD 15 per student, for 3–5 students. A package of four individual 60-minute lessons costs USD 90. A 20-minute introductory consultation is free; it is not a tutoring lesson. The stated prices are in USD and no other discounts or taxes are specified. Tutoring inquiries and consultations are subject to staff confirmation; the chatbot cannot confirm actual availability or book a session.

Service hours are Monday–Saturday, 10:00–19:00 Beirut local time, with Sundays closed. Customers may request sessions through hello@cedarbridge.example or by voluntarily leaving their name, email, and tutoring needs with the chatbot for local demo logging. Requests should ideally be made at least 24 hours before the desired session. A confirmed lesson can be rescheduled without a fee with at least 24 hours' notice; last-minute cancellations within 24 hours normally forfeit that session. If CedarBridge cancels a confirmed lesson, it offers a reschedule or refund for the affected session. Other refunds require human review. No payments or refunds are handled by the chatbot.

Customer privacy: contact information is only logged if the customer explicitly gives permission. For this demo, lead requests are written to a local logs/leads.jsonl file and unresolved questions or complaints are written to logs/feedback.jsonl. These logs are not automatically forwarded to a real person; the .example email address is illustrative and cannot receive actual messages.
